Chapter 475: Even If We Meet Again, We Would Not Recognize Each Other (Lu Yuandi Side Story)

Prev Chapter Next Chapter

Lu Yuandi held a persimmon in her hand, gazing at the misty, cloud-veiled mountains before her.

It resembled Peach Blossom Mountain, yet it was not. This mountain held not a trace of immortal energy-only an endless sense of void and desolation.

She still remembered Tao Mian carrying her on his back to her hometown, that tombstone prepared for her finally put to use. Life had reached its end, and the one beside her was the person she could least bear to part with. This brought Lu Yuandi immeasurable peace.

Back then, the great general Li had cursed her, saying she had chosen the wrong path. A path destined to grow ever narrower, a lifetime of cold solitude-and told her never to look back.

Lu Yuandi had indeed tasted that bitterness, all the way to the very end.

Lying on Tao Mian’s back, Lu Yuandi recalled her childhood. She had been playful, gathering nuts in the mountains until it grew too late, curling up in a tree hollow and falling asleep.
